0–3s — Emotional hook
“What’s a memory you never want to lose?”

3–6s — Pick a prompt
Show the Heritage question card:
“What was a family recipe that only they could get right?”
Tap Answer.

6–9s — Choose a person
Open Who is this about?
Several people rise into view with the app’s existing staggered animation.
Select Grandma Eleanor · Grandmother.

9–16s — Speak or write
The composer opens. Tap Dictate, speak, stop, and show Transcribing… before the words appear. Brief cursor motion shows the text can also be edited manually.
"Every Sunday, Grandma Eleanor filled the kitchen with warm bread and stories from home."

16–19s — Preserve it
Enter Sunday Bread and tap Save story.

19–24s — Read the memory
Open Grandma Eleanor’s saved story and let the audience see the readable story page.

24–27s — Hear the memory
Tap Listen; it changes to Pause, and we hear one short sentence.

27–30s — End card
“Read, listen, and hold onto the moments that matter.”
