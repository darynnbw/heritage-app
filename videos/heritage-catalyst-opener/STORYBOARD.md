## Video direction

**Layout**: Warm, editorial paper canvas. A large vertical phone container (70–80% viewport height) acts as the central hero prop, holding the UI.
**Motion**: Smooth, tactile UI interactions. Focus on spring-based taps, swipes, and precise transitions between screens without feeling hyperactive.
**Typography**: Lora for memory text; SF Pro for UI elements.
**Brand**: Slate ink on paper white, with teal (#0f766e) accents.
