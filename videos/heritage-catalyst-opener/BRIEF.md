## Intent

A 30-second opener video for the Catalyst demo before the slide deck. Shows a complete journey from a simple prompt to a preserved, listenable family story. Warm, editorial, intimate style using the real Heritage identity.

## Assets

- `d:\heritage\src\mvp\mvp.css` — Canonical source for styles
- `d:\heritage\design-system\heritage\MASTER.md` — Canonical source for brand tokens
- Existing Heritage SVG illustrations and Lucide icons

## Customizations

- The video is 16:9 for the event screen, but a large vertical phone (70-80% height) remains the visual hero.
- Animated versions of existing component interactions—tap compression, prompt swapping, dictation, saving, and listening.
- Use actual UI components: real prompt cards, buttons, People cards, person selector, Dictate control, Listen control, and mobile tab bar.
- Minimal spoken audio (one short human voice during dictation: "Every Sunday, Grandma Eleanor filled the kitchen with warm bread and stories from home.")
- Subtle tap, card, and save sounds.
- Final Tagline: "Read, listen, and hold onto the moments that matter."

## Notes

- Palette: paper white, slate ink, teal #0f766e, pale teal, soft gray surfaces.
- Typography: Lora for story titles/quotes and SF Pro/system sans stack for UI.
- Avoid the older gold/dark .interface-design direction.
